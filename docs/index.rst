Fubo TV makes it easy to access your account and start watching your favorite live TV channels, sports, news, and entertainment content.

If you already have a Fubo TV account, you can sign in using your registered email address and password.

This guide explains how to login to your Fubo TV account, access your account on different devices, and troubleshoot common login problems.

How to Login Fubo TV Account?
-----------------------------

Follow these simple steps to sign in to your Fubo TV account:

**Step 1: Open Fubo TV**

Open a supported web browser on your computer or mobile device and visit the official Fubo TV website.

**Step 2: Select Sign In**

Look for the **Sign In** option on the Fubo TV website and select it to open the login page.

**Step 3: Enter Your Email**

Enter the email address associated with your Fubo TV account in the required field.

**Step 4: Enter Your Password**

Type your Fubo TV account password carefully. Make sure Caps Lock is not enabled if your password contains uppercase and lowercase letters.

**Step 5: Select Sign In**

After entering your login details, select **Sign In**. If the information is correct, you should be taken to your Fubo TV account.

How to Login Fubo TV on Smart TV?
---------------------------------

You can also sign in to Fubo TV through the app installed on a compatible Smart TV.

**Step 1:** Open the Fubo TV app on your Smart TV.

**Step 2:** Select the **Sign In** option.

**Step 3:** Choose the available login method.

**Step 4:** If an activation code appears on your TV screen, follow the instructions shown on the screen to complete the sign-in process.

**Step 5:** Once authentication is completed, your Fubo TV account should become available on the TV.

How to Login Fubo TV on Roku?
-----------------------------

To access Fubo TV on Roku, open the Fubo TV app from your Roku device and select **Sign In**. Follow the on-screen instructions to enter your account information or complete the activation process.

Make sure your Roku device has a stable internet connection before attempting to sign in.

How to Login Fubo TV on Fire TV?
--------------------------------

Open the Fubo TV application on your Fire TV device and select **Sign In**. Enter your account credentials when prompted. If the app provides an activation option, follow the instructions displayed on your TV.

After successful authentication, you can start using Fubo TV on your Fire TV device.

Fubo TV Login Not Working?
---------------------------

Sometimes, users may experience problems while trying to sign in. Try the following solutions if your Fubo TV login is not working.

**Check Your Email and Password**

Make sure that your email address and password are entered correctly. Even a small typing mistake can prevent you from signing in.

**Reset Your Password**

If you cannot remember your password, use the password recovery option available on the Fubo TV login page and follow the instructions to create a new password.

**Check Your Internet Connection**

A weak or unstable internet connection can cause login pages or apps to load incorrectly. Restart your router or connect your device to a more reliable network.

**Update the Fubo TV App**

If you are using Fubo TV on a Smart TV, Roku, Fire TV, or another supported device, make sure the application is updated to the latest available version.

**Restart Your Device**

Restarting your streaming device can resolve temporary application or connection problems. Close the Fubo TV app, restart your device, and try signing in again.

**Clear Browser Cache**

If you are signing in through a web browser, clearing cached data and cookies may help resolve loading or login problems.

Forgot Fubo TV Account Password?
--------------------------------

If you have forgotten your Fubo TV password, use the password recovery option on the sign-in page. Enter the email associated with your account and follow the instructions provided to reset your password.

After creating a new password, return to the login page and try signing in again.

Tips for a Successful Fubo TV Login
-----------------------------------

* Use the correct email address associated with your Fubo TV account.
* Enter your password carefully.
* Keep your Fubo TV application updated.
* Use a stable internet connection.
* Restart your streaming device if the app becomes unresponsive.
* Avoid entering incorrect login information repeatedly.
* Use the password recovery option if you cannot remember your password.

Frequently Asked Questions
---------------------------

**Why can't I login to my Fubo TV account?**

Incorrect account credentials, connectivity problems, outdated applications, or temporary technical issues can prevent successful login. Check your credentials and internet connection before trying again.

**Can I login to Fubo TV on a Smart TV?**

Yes. You can open the Fubo TV application on a supported Smart TV and use the available sign-in instructions.

**What should I do if I forgot my Fubo TV password?**

Use the password recovery option on the Fubo TV sign-in page and follow the instructions to reset your password.

**Why is the Fubo TV app not opening the login page?**

Check your internet connection, restart the application and device, and make sure the Fubo TV app is updated.

**Can I use my Fubo TV account on multiple devices?**

Fubo TV supports access across compatible devices, although simultaneous streaming and other account features can depend on your subscription and current service policies.
